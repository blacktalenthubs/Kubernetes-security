# Search and reach

How captions actually get found, and the hashtag banks to draw from.

## Where the platforms read keywords

Both apps now work partly as search engines, and a growing share of reach comes
from someone typing a phrase rather than scrolling a feed. That is good news for
her, because search traffic keeps working weeks after posting while feed reach
dies in a day.

The indexed slots, roughly in order of value:

| Slot | Instagram | TikTok | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| First line of caption | high | high | Truncates ~125 chars on IG. Keyword goes early. |
| Spoken audio | no | **high** | TikTok transcribes video. Most-missed slot by far. |
| On-screen text | some | high | Also the cover frame people decide on. |
| Hashtags | medium | medium | Fewer and more relevant beats a wall. |
| Alt text | medium | n/a | Manually set on IG. Free keyword slot, almost nobody uses it. |
| Rest of caption | medium | medium | Secondary phrases live here. |

The practical upshot: **for a talking video, the words she says out loud matter
as much as the caption.** A script that never says "high protein african
dinner" cannot rank for it, no matter what the caption says.

## Choosing the phrase

Aim at what someone types when they do not already know she exists. They are
not searching her name; they are searching a problem or a craving.

Search intent in her lanes tends to look like:

- **Meals** - `healthy dinner ideas`, `high protein dinner`, `what i eat in a
  day`, `easy healthy meals`, `[ingredient] recipe`
- **African food + fitness** - `high protein african food`, `healthy nigerian
  food`, `african food macros`, `jollof rice calories`
- **Fitness** - `fitness meal prep`, `losing weight while eating carbs`,
  `beginner gym routine`, `fit mom routine`
- **Fashion** - `gym outfit ideas`, `casual outfit inspo`, `[body type] outfit
  ideas`, `outfit for [occasion]`

Pick one primary phrase per post. Two posts targeting the same phrase compete
with each other; targeting adjacent ones covers more ground.

## The compounding play

The strongest growth pattern for a creator in her position is a **repeatable
searchable series** - the same format, same phrase family, posted regularly.
"What I eat in a day on my program" as a recurring post trains the algorithm on
what she is about and stacks results in search over time, where one-off posts
do not.

Worth suggesting when the topic naturally repeats. Not worth forcing.

## Hashtag sets

Keep her shape - about five, lowercase, at the end - but vary the sizes. A set
where every tag has 10M+ posts buries her in all of them. A set where every tag
is tiny reaches nobody. Mix:

- **1 broad** (1M+ posts) - a lottery ticket, occasionally pays
- **2-3 mid** (100k-1M) - where she can realistically rank
- **1-2 niche** (under 100k) - where she can rank today
- **1 identity** - `#fitmom` and its relatives. This is her people.

Rotate sets between posts. Identical tags on every post is a weak signal and
flattens which audiences get tested.

about a specific dish, the dish name as a tag will usually outperform anything
listed here, because that is what people search.

## Alt text

Instagram lets her set it manually, most people never do, and it is indexed.
One plain sentence describing what is in the image, with the keyword in it.
Write it as a description, not a caption - it is read aloud by screen readers,
so it should actually describe the photo.

Good: `Plate of Japanese sweet potatoes, salmon in sauce, and sliced avocado on
a white plate.`
Bad: `Dinner vibes ✨ #healthy`

## What not to do for reach

- **Keyword stuffing.** A caption that lists phrases instead of saying
  something reads as spam to a person even if a machine likes it, and the
  person is the one who decides to follow.
- **Thirty-tag walls.** Diminishing returns, and off-voice for her.
- **Banned or shadow-flagged tags.** Very generic ones like `#followforfollow`
  or `#likeforlike` can suppress a post's reach.
- **Chasing a trend phrase she would not say.** It dates the post and costs the
  voice, which is the actual asset.
