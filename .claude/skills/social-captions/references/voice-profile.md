## Patterns observed in the samples

Drawn from the captions above. Update as more come in.

**Shape**
- Very short. A header line, then items, one per line.
- A blank line between every line. The caption is a list with air in it, not a
  paragraph.
- Header is a light frame for what follows: "On Today's Dinner ✨". Title-ish
  capitalisation on the header, plain capitalisation on the items.

**Word choice**
- Plain nouns, no adjectives. "Salmon sauce." "Avocado." She names the thing
  and moves on. No "creamy," "perfectly seared," "vibrant," "nourishing."
- Slightly loose grammar, and it should stay loose. "Salmon sauce" is not a
  standard dish name, and cleaning it up to "salmon in a cream sauce" would
  make it sound like a menu instead of like her.
- She does not explain, sell, or justify the food. It is on the plate, so she
  lists it.

**What she does not do**
- No call to action. No "recipe below," no "save this," no "tell me in the
  comments."
- No lesson or takeaway line at the end.
- No hook question at the top.
- No emoji spray. One sparkle on the header line, and that is the whole budget.

**Hashtags**
- Five, lowercase, no camel case, at the end of the caption body.
- The shape is roughly three topic tags, one lifestyle tag, one identity tag:
  `#healthydinner #healthymealideas #healthymeal` then `#healthyliving` then
  `#fitmom`.
- `#fitmom` is the identity tag and it is doing real work. She is a mom, and
  that is the audience that finds her. Keep an identity tag in every set.

## Spoken voice

From a voice note, for teleprompter scripts. This is looser than her writing
and should stay that way.

- Repeats a word for emphasis rather than reaching for a stronger word.
- Uses "so" to start and to connect.
- Thinks out loud, leaves sentences unfinished, uses filler like "mhmm."
- Talks in concrete terms - calories, results, what a food does - not in
  abstractions about wellness or journeys.

A script that reads as complete, grammatical sentences will sound read. Leave
the fragments in.
